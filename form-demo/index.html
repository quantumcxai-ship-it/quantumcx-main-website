What happens when someone presses send

  1. The enquiry is stored first. Before any email is attempted. If mail breaks at three in the morning, the enquiry is still there in the morning.
  2. Then you are emailed. Name, phone, message, the page they were on, and the time in your timezone.
  3. Reply straight from your inbox. The reply address is the enquirer's, not ours. You never sign in to anything.
  4. Bots are dropped silently. A hidden field no human can fill, and a limit on how fast one site can submit.
  5. Your key only works on your domain. Lifted and pasted elsewhere, it is refused.
